MODERN PERMING

Texture, movement
and lasting shape.

Whether you’re looking for soft beachy waves, effortless movement, defined curls or added volume, a modern perm can completely transform your hair.

Today’s perming techniques create natural-looking texture tailored to your individual style, hair type and lifestyle. Specialist products and rods carefully restructure the hair to create a long-lasting curl or wave.

Appointment time

Perm appointments are detailed, bespoke services and typically take between 2½ and 4½ hours, depending on your hair length, thickness and desired result.

WHAT TO EXPECT

Every stage,
carefully considered.

  1. 01A thorough consultation to discuss your desired result.
  2. 02Preparation and careful sectioning of the hair.
  3. 03Precise winding of the perm rods.
  4. 04Application and processing of the perm solution.
  5. 05Neutralising to lock in the new shape.
  6. 06A professional finish, styling advice and aftercare.
CONSULTATION REQUIRED

Healthy hair comes first.

Every perm begins with a mandatory consultation. We’ll assess your hair’s condition, discuss its history, talk through your expectations and confirm whether a perm is suitable.

Once your consultation is complete, you’ll receive a personalised quotation before your appointment, so you know exactly what to expect.
