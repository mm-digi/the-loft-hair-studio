Your autumn
hair refresh.

There is something about autumn that makes everyone want a change. The wardrobe shifts to richer tones, the evenings get cosier, and your hair deserves a refresh too. Whether you are after a whole new look or simply want to bring some warmth and shine back after the summer, this is the season to treat yourself. Here is what we are loving at The Loft this autumn.

Warm, rich colours are having a moment

Summer is all about bright, sun-kissed tones, but autumn calls for something deeper and warmer. Think rich coppers, warm chestnuts, golden caramels and glossy chocolate browns, shades that catch beautifully in the softer autumn light and sit well with the season. If you have been blonde all summer, adding some warmth through the mid-lengths and ends is a lovely way to transition without a dramatic overhaul.

Repair and restore after summer

Sun, sea and heat styling can leave hair feeling a little worse for wear by the time autumn arrives. This is the ideal moment for a nourishing treatment to restore condition, lock in moisture and get your hair looking healthy and glossy again. A refresh is not only about colour and cut. Sometimes the biggest transformation comes from simply getting your hair back to its best.

Styles to see you into the new season

Autumn is a lovely time to try something a little different. A soft, face-framing cut, a fresh fringe, or some gentle layers for movement can lift your look without a big change. With party season quietly approaching, it is also a smart time to get a shape you love locked in, so you are ready as the invitations start arriving.

Why book in now

As the run-up to Christmas approaches, the diary fills up quickly. It is always our busiest time of year. Booking your autumn appointment now means you get the slot that suits you, and it sets you up for that pre-party appointment later on. A little forward planning goes a long way.

Treat yourself this autumn

Whether it is a bold new colour, a nourishing treatment or simply a trim and a bit of pampering, we would love to look after you. Book your appointment at The Loft Hair Studio today and let us find your autumn look together.
